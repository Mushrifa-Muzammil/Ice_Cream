# 🍦 IceWorld - Premium Ice Cream Shop

IceWorld is a fully functional, responsive, and visually stunning e-commerce website for an ice cream parlor. Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, it offers a seamless shopping experience with a modern, colorful design. Customers can browse popular and most-selling ice creams, add items to cart, like products, earn loyalty points, and place orders with delivery to Siyambalagaskotuwa, Kurunegala, Sri Lanka.

screen-short.png

## ✨ Features

- **Online Shopping System** – Add products to cart, adjust quantities, and remove items.
- **Wishlist (Like)** – Heart icon on each product to save favorites; view them in a dedicated sidebar.
- **User Authentication (Mock)** – Simple login/signup modals (any email/password works).
- **Order History** – After logging in, view your past orders with status and details.
- **Loyalty Points** – Earn 10 points for every $1 spent; points are displayed in the navbar.
- **Location Pre-filled** – Checkout form automatically fills delivery address as Siyambalagaskotuwa, Kurunegala, Sri Lanka.
- **Countdown Timers** – Limited-time offers with live countdowns.
- **Image Gallery** – Beautiful grid showcasing ice cream creations and shop ambiance.
- **Our Shop Section** – Photos of the shop exterior, interior, and happy staff.
- **Responsive Design** – Works flawlessly on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- **Scroll Animations** – Smooth reveal effects using ScrollReveal library.
- **Persistent Data** – Cart, wishlist, orders, and points are saved in browser's localStorage.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5** – Semantic markup
- **CSS3** – Custom properties, gradients, animations, responsive design
- **JavaScript (ES6)** – Dynamic interactions, localStorage management
- **RemixIcon** – Beautiful icon set for UI elements
- **ScrollReveal** – Library for scroll-based animations
- **Unsplash & Flaticon** – Free stock images and icons

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)

### Installation
1. Download or clone the repository.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Double-click `index.html` to launch the website in your default browser.

No build tools or server required – it's pure frontend.

## 📖 How to Use

- **Browse Products** – Scroll through the Popular and Most Selling sections.
- **Add to Cart** – Click the "Add to Cart" button on any product.
- **Like a Product** – Click the heart icon (♡) on a product card; it turns red (❤️) and saves to your wishlist.
- **View Cart** – Click the cart icon in the navbar to open the cart sidebar; adjust quantities or remove items.
- **View Wishlist** – Click the heart icon in the navbar to open your liked items.
- **Checkout** – In the cart sidebar, click "Checkout", fill in your details (address is pre-filled), choose delivery time, and confirm order.
- **Login / Signup** – Click the user icon in the navbar; use any email/password (mock authentication). After login, click the user icon again to see your order history.
- **Loyalty Points** – Earned automatically with each order; displayed in the navbar.
- **Offers** – See the countdown timers for limited-time deals in the Offers section.
